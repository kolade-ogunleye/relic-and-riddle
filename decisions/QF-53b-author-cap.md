# QF-53b: do automatic paths trim pools that already exist to meet the 35% author cap?

Raised by the independent review of QF-53b round 2 (queue/QF-53b.review-2.md, blocking 1): once the diversity
rules work on real pools, a forced review trims the 16 vision-ready dev pools from 211 photos to 168 (Antikythera
13 -> 6, Hal Saflieni 11 -> 6) and a refill with no new photos moves 73 photos to reserve (ready 17 -> 14).

Options put to the operator: (1) quotas govern only what the fetcher adds, existing pool photos never move out
automatically, author share and shot-class balance are notices, near-duplicates still removed; (2) trim existing
pools to 35%; (3) hold pools whole until they meet 35%.

answer: option 1, chosen by the operator on 2026-11-09 (in the Claude session). This narrows QF-53's acceptance
"max author share <= 35% per approved pool" to pools built or refilled by the fetcher; a pool's existing photos are
never trimmed for a quota by automatic review or refill, and its share is reported.
