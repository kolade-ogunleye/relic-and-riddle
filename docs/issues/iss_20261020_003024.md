## User Critique
The horizontal line separating card titles from the body extends beyond the first line, and the second-line burst is awkward. Also, could we use different formatting styles, such as fonts, size, bold, italics, underlines, etc., where applicable, to make it easier to read and improve the UI/UX on Telegram?

## Antigravity (AGY) Autonomous Diagnosis & Resolution
1. **Diagnosis**:
   - The card title divider was formatted using 20 consecutive box-drawing unicode characters `━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━`. In Telegram's proportional mobile font renderer, these characters exceeded the message bubble width on small and standard mobile screens, causing 2-4 characters to wrap awkwardly onto a second line ("second-line burst").
   - Card content lacked clear typographic hierarchy (underlines, italics, monospace badges).
2. **Resolution**:
   - Replaced all 20-character unicode dividers with `<code>──────────────</code>` (14 monospace box-drawing characters). In fixed-width `<code>`, 14 characters occupy less than 110px width—guaranteeing they never overflow or burst into a second line on any mobile device.
   - Elevated typography and formatting styles across cards:
     - **Cockpit Dashboard**: `🏛️ <b><u>RELIC &amp; RIDDLE</u></b> • <i>Executive Cockpit</i>`, with monospace numbers `<code>{len(queued)}</code>`, italicized status states `<i>{sched_state}</i>`, and underline accents.
     - **Production Progress**: `⏳ <b><u>Rendering Production</u>:</b>\n<b>{topic_title}</b> • <code>[{run_id}]</code>`, italicized active step, and monospace timing stats `<code>{elapsed_fmt}</code>`.
     - **Topic Queue**: `📋 <b><u>Topic Queue</u></b> (<i>{len(queued)} queued</i>)` with clean underline sectioning.
     - **Review Master Card**: `🎬 <b><u>Studio Master Ready for Review</u></b>`, italicized location, and monospace word counts and audio mastering badges.
3. **Verification**:
   - All 58 unit tests across `tests/test_control.py`, `tests/test_music.py`, and `tests/test_ui_overhaul.py` pass with 100% success rate.
