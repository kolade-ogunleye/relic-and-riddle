## User Critique
Can we use more intuitive, precise, and concise card and notification titles? Can we use new lines to separate sections and titles from the body?

## Antigravity (AGY) Autonomous Diagnosis & Resolution
1. **Diagnosis**:
   - **Verbose Titles**:
     - Previous card and template headers included verbose, redundant descriptors:
       - `RELIC & RIDDLE EXECUTIVE COCKPIT` -> bloated
       - `STUDIO MASTER READY FOR REVIEW` -> wordy
       - `RENDERING PRODUCTION` -> clunky phrasing
       - `ZERO-TRUST AUDIT LOG` -> needlessly technical
       - `RECENT ALERTS & LOGS` -> redundant
       - `... COMMAND TEMPLATE` / `... SELECTION` -> repetitive postfix across 7 command helpers
   - **Missing Vertical Breathing Room**:
     - When horizontal divider lines (`<code>──────────────</code>`) were previously removed, titles were left separated from card bodies by only a single newline (`\n`), causing the title and the first line of body text to visually touch with no whitespace padding.
     - Section blocks within notification and review cards were similarly dense, lacking clean double-newline (`\n\n`) paragraph breaks.
2. **Resolution**:
   - **Intuitive, Precise & Concise Titles**:
     - Upgraded all card, template, and alert titles to clean, punchy uppercase underlined identifiers:
       - Cockpit: `🏛️ <b><u>EXECUTIVE COCKPIT</u></b>`
       - Queue: `📋 <b><u>TOPIC QUEUE</u></b>`
       - Master Review: `🎬 <b><u>MASTER REVIEW</u></b>`
       - Progress: `⏳ <b><u>PRODUCTION PROGRESS</u></b>`
       - Audit Log: `🛡️ <b><u>AUDIT LOG</u></b>`
       - Alerts: `🔔 <b><u>SYSTEM ALERTS</u></b>`
       - Touch & Template Helpers: `⬆ <b><u>BUMP TOPIC</u></b>`, `🎬 <b><u>STORYBOARD REVIEW</u></b>`, `🗑 <b><u>DROP IMAGE</u></b>`, `🔄 <b><u>SWAP BEATS</u></b>`, `➕ <b><u>ADD TOPIC</u></b>`, `🛠️ <b><u>REPORT ISSUE</u></b>`
       - Operational Notifications: `🧹 <b><u>CHAT CLEARED</u></b>`, `⚠️ <b><u>TOPIC SKIPPED: FACT-STARVED</u></b>`, `❌ <b><u>PRODUCTION FAILED</u></b>`, `🔁 <b><u>VOICE FALLBACK</u></b>`, `⏰ <b><u>REVIEW TIMEOUT</u></b>`, `🗓 <b><u>POST SCHEDULED</u></b>`, `🛑 <b><u>BROADCAST BLOCKED: UNLICENSED VOICE</u></b>`, `⚠️ <b><u>PUBLISH UNCONFIRMED</u></b>`, `❌ <b><u>PUBLISH FAILED</u></b>`, `✅ <b><u>PUBLISHED</u></b>`
   - **Double-Newline Structural Separation**:
     - Added double newlines (`\n\n`) between card titles and the card body across all cards, templates, and operational notifications.
     - Inserted clean blank lines (`\n\n`) between major semantic sections (metrics, script excerpts, debate polls, technical specs) to maximize readability and ergonomics on mobile screens.
